1. Reviewing a session

  1. Load the session. Drop the day's inspection report under Begin review and pick the session, or drop the session's files by hand under Or load files directly. Files can be dropped in several batches; each drop adds to what is loaded.
  2. Check the pre-filled scores. Radar and file-based scores fill in automatically. They are suggestions marked auto-suggested.
  3. Watch the video for each scenario and episode, and change any score the footage contradicts.
  4. Answer the manual items: PII, moderator visible, audio/video quality if needed.
  5. Flag or comment on anything unusual at its timestamp.
  6. Make the session decision (Pass or Fail) in the banner at the top.
  7. Save. Save writes every form you touched plus the session decision. It will not save until a decision is chosen.

2. How scoring works

Every item has a priority. Each item is turned into a percentage, then averaged with the other items of the same priority.

PriorityPass lineMeaning
Gate—Pass/fail on its own. A failed gate fails the form outright, whatever the averages say. Sync Chirp (C0) and PII visible (episode) are gates.
P0average ≥ 85%Must-have items. A meter item's pass line is drawn on the meter (Continuous Movement passes at 75%); a value on its line counts as exactly passing.
P1average ≥ 75%Important items.

A form passes when no gate failed, its P0 average is ≥ 85% and its P1 average is ≥ 75%. Empty items are left out of the averages.

How each kind of answer becomes a percentage

  • 1 / 2 / 3 scale — 1 = 33%, 2 = 67%, 3 = 100%. 3 is reserved for great. One 2 on a P0 item usually pulls the P0 average below 85%.
  • 0–100 meter — the value itself, with the pass line drawn on the meter. A small triangle shows where the radar put it.
  • Yes / No — Yes = 100%, No = 0%.
  • Counts (Device Placements) — 6 positions sits exactly on the 85% line; more is higher, fewer is lower.

3. C0 calibration items

C0 is one ~55 s episode of smooth whole-rig motion recorded once per session. C0 must pass for the session to pass. The runbook asks for varied smooth motion, not an exact path: a different loop count, gentle tilt or imperfect plane is not by itself a failure.

ItemPre-filled fromHow to judge
Sync Chirp Gatesession_evidence.json: pass only if camera detection, ambiMIK detection and alignment all pass.Fail fails C0 and therefore the session. Only override if the evidence file is clearly wrong, and say why in the notes.
Static (start) P0Radar: how much the rig moved in the first 5 s of the episode. 3 if ≤ 10%, 2 if ≤ 40%.Was the rig held still before motion began?
Figure-8 motion P0Radar: how much of the middle of the episode the rig was moving. 3 if ≥ 50%, 2 if ≥ 25%.The radar only sees that the rig moved, not the shape. Confirm smooth looping motion on the video.
Translation P0Same radar reading as Figure-8.Confirm forward/back, left/right and up/down movement on the video.
Static (end) P0Radar: rig motion in the last 5 s. Same cut points as Static (start).Was the rig set down and still before End Episode?

Rig-motion suggestions need the C0 .mmwave.bin together with its capture.json, episode markers and mmwave sync JSON.

6. Justifying a score

The pre-filled numbers are measurements, not verdicts. Keep them unless the video shows they are wrong. When you change one, or make a borderline call, write a note in the episode's Reviewer notes so the next person can follow your reasoning.

A good note says three things:
  1. What you saw, with a timestamp.
  2. What you changed, from what to what.
  3. Why — the rule it relates to.
0:12–0:40 participant sits on the sofa; radar lost them. Continuous Movement 41% → 70%: they were moving the rest of the time.
Speed Variance 3 → 2: same walking pace for most of the episode, only one change at 0:35.
PII Yes: unconsented passer-by's face at 0:22, clearly visible for 3 s. Flagged.
  • Change a score when the video clearly contradicts it: the radar followed the wrong person, lost someone for a long stretch, or the footage shows something the files cannot (PII, the moderator, picture quality).
  • Do not change a score just to get an episode over the line. If it is close, leave it and say so in the note.
  • A changed radar value shows adjusted · radar N, so the original stays visible.
  • If the radar note says it tracked only a small share of frames, treat its numbers as weak and lean on the video.

7. Flags and comments

Both are pinned to a moment in the video. Use the button while the video is at that moment.

⚑ Flag a moment when

  • PII is visible
  • an unconsented person appears
  • the rig moves or is touched during an episode
  • someone lingers outside the view past 3 m
  • video or audio drops, freezes or is unusable
  • anything unsafe happens

💬 Comment when

  • a later reviewer needs context ("operator walks through at 0:32")
  • it explains a score you changed
  • you made a borderline call

Keep each one to a sentence. Flags and comments save immediately; they do not need the Save button.

8. Rooms and the session decision

LevelRule
EpisodePasses when no gate failed (PII = No), P0 ≥ 85% and P1 ≥ 75%.
Room (T1)Kept when its technical items pass and it has at least 10 passing episodes. Otherwise rejected. Episodes the operator excluded in Hydra never count.
SessionPasses when C0 passes and at least one T1 room is kept. Rejected rooms are dropped. If the session has only one T1 room, that room being rejected fails the session.

The banner at the top shows the app's Recommended result and why. A ✕ on a room pill means that room is rejected. Your decision (Pass or Fail) is what counts: it is saved as the session's result and goes to the production tracker, where the team decides whether to deliver the session. Choose it after reviewing; Save will not run without it.

If you decide against the recommendation, say why in a comment or a reviewer note.

9. Reset, clear and troubleshooting

  • Reset scores to radar sets aside every saved score and the session decision for the loaded session, and refills the forms from the radar and the files. Notes and flags are kept. Nothing changes in SharePoint until you Save; reload the page to back out.
  • Clear local cache removes this browser's backup copy of the session only. Use it after rows were deleted in SharePoint directly.
  • A value looks impossible (for example 3% movement on someone clearly walking): it is probably an old saved value from before a scoring change. Use Reset scores to radar.
  • "Downloads were blocked … CORS": the blob storage is not yet set up for this site. Drop the files by hand instead.
  • No radar scores for a room: the room needs its .mmwave.bin, episode markers and mmwave sync JSON. The load log says which file is missing.
